## The tension curve

*Plot the stake column. Look at the shape, not the numbers.*

```
5 |
4 |
3 |
2 |
1 |
  +----------------------------------------
    1    2    3    4    5    6
```

**Diagnostics:**

- [ ] Does it reach 4+ within the first 60 seconds? *(Law 2 — no gap, no attention)*
- [ ] Is it flat anywhere for more than ~90 seconds? *(the most common structural defect)*
- [ ] Does it peak at or near the end, not in the middle?
- [ ] Is there variation, or is it a plateau at 3?

*A flat line means the audience has no reason to keep listening. Fix it here — it is
nearly impossible to fix with better sentences later.*
